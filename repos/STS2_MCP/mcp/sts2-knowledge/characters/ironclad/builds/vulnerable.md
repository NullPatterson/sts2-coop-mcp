# Vulnerable (Ironclad)

**Goal:** Apply Vulnerable (enemies take 50% more damage while afflicted) and cash it in
with strong attacks. The most approachable Ironclad build — the payoff is obvious as
soon as you have both halves (a Vulnerable source and a big attack to follow it with),
and the starter deck already includes one Vulnerable card (Bash).

**Early priority cards** (grab these as soon as offered):
- Uppercut — reliable Vulnerable application at a fair cost; one of the safest early picks.
- Taunt — block plus wide Vulnerable, a hybrid card that covers two problems at once.
- Molten Fist — doubles up your Vulnerable effect when paired with other sources.
- Tremble — applies Vulnerable across two turns for one Energy; unglamorous but efficient.

**Consistency cards** (once you can apply Vulnerable reliably):
- Dismantle — cheap and, upgraded, hits for 20 damage for one Energy once the deck can
  set it up.
- Dominate — an alternate win condition for long elite/boss fights: pairs Vulnerable with
  Strength so the deck isn't purely reliant on the debuff.
- Cruelty — ends fights quickly once Vulnerable is up; you rarely need to stack huge
  amounts of it since most fights end in well under five turns.

**Key relics:**
- Red Skull — 3 Strength while at or below 50% HP; a Vulnerable deck takes damage, so this
  turns that cost into a bonus.
- Self-Forming Clay — 3 Block next turn whenever you lose HP in combat; offsets the same cost.
- Paper Phrog — the single best relic for this build: Vulnerable enemies take 75% more
  damage instead of 50%.
- Ruined Helmet — situational (you don't need Strength cards for Vulnerable to work), but
  transformative if the run also gives you a Strength line.

**Playstyle notes:** Watch how many attack cards you're accumulating — it's easy to end up
with a hand that's all offense and no block. Balance every couple of Vulnerable/attack
picks with a defensive card so you don't get caught overcommitted.
