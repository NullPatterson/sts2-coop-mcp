# Body Slam (Ironclad)

**Goal:** Stack large amounts of Block, then convert it directly into damage with Body
Slam. The most forgiving Ironclad line — exceptional survivability means you can grind
out wins even without a perfect draft, only attacking when the opportunity is there.

**Early priority cards:**
- Shrug It Off — deceptively strong: Block and card draw in one card, exactly when you need both.
- Body Slam — the build's namesake payoff; deals damage equal to your current Block.
- Blood Wall — a big single block source to fuel Body Slam turns.
- Flame Barrier — defensive card that punishes attackers, another block source for the build.

**Consistency cards** (once you're offered a pivot into this build, often not obvious
until Act 2):
- Unmovable — turns even basic Defends into strong defensive cards.
- Crimson Mantle, Impervious — big defensive tools that, combined with Blood Wall and
  Flame Barrier, let you stop almost any single attack.

**Key relics** (there are more good fits for this build than almost any other):
- Bronze Scales — 3 Thorns at combat start; can end some fights on its own via reflected damage.
- Gorget, Oddly Smooth Stone — Plating and Dexterity respectively, both gain you Block
  right as a fight starts.
- Anchor, Horn Cleat, Captain's Wheel — Block on your first three turns, letting you use
  Body Slam without spending a card on Block first.
- Parrying Shield — 6 damage to a random enemy at end of turn if you have 10+ Block —
  nearly guaranteed with this build.

**Playstyle notes:** This build doesn't need a lucky draft to work — the defensive
backbone alone wins fights slowly and safely. Attack when a real opportunity appears
rather than forcing it; the deck's job is to stay alive long enough that Body Slam
eventually does the work.
