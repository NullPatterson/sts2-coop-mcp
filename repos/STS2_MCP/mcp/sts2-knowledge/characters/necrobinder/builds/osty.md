# Osty (Necrobinder)

**Goal:** Grow Osty (your Summon companion) from a 1-HP minion into a boss-level ally
that can one-shot enemies with a single card. The easiest Necrobinder build to pick up —
Osty isn't affected by most debuffs or buffs you receive, and it soaks damage you'd
otherwise take once your Block runs out, functioning as a second health pool.

**Early priority cards:**
- Unleash — your primary Osty damage tool from the starting deck; upgrades into
  Protector, the strongest Osty payoff card in the pool.
- Bodyguard, Afterlife, Spur, Necro Mastery — Summon cards that bolster Osty so it can
  survive encounters without being reduced to 1 HP.

**Consistency cards** (this build wants a Souls package alongside it for the same
cycling reasons described in the Souls build):
- Fetch, Flatten, Right Hand Hand, Rattle — a real combo: Fetch attacks first (enabling
  Flatten's cost reduction to zero), then Rattle finishes for a total of ~29 damage for 1
  Energy. With enough Souls-driven cycling, you can chain this multiple times in one turn.
- Squeeze — an alternate win condition when you can't keep Osty alive long enough for a
  strong Unleash; deals a guaranteed minimum and can scale to hundreds of damage in a long fight.

**Key relics:**
- Bone Flute — 2 Block whenever Osty attacks; an obvious include.
- Bookmark — reduces the cost of a Retained card at end of turn; strong if your deck runs
  Retain cards.
- Funerary Mask — good in any Necrobinder deck, grab it regardless of build.

**Playstyle notes:** Use Friendship and Shared Fate deliberately to redirect buffs away
from yourself and toward Osty when it benefits the matchup — Osty ignoring most
debuffs/buffs is a real asymmetry worth building around, not just a footnote.
