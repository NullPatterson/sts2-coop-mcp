# Souls (Necrobinder)

**Goal:** Souls are a Necrobinder-unique card type — a free (0-Energy) Skill that draws
2 cards and Exhausts. Cycling through your deck this fast lets you reach your best cards
without waiting several turns for them to show up, and Souls cards are generally
strong on their own merits, making this build useful in nearly every Necrobinder deck.

**Early priority cards:**
- Grave Warden, Reave — not individually exciting, but they generate the Souls that
  power your draw engine.
- Graveblast — not a Souls card itself, but lets you replay your main cards, making it
  strong at every stage of a run.

**Consistency cards** (once your draw engine is active):
- Capture Spirit — a cheap way to add Souls to your deck.
- Dirge — pairs extremely well with Borrowed Time.
- Severance — keeps your damage output high while filling out the Souls package.
- Haunt — start looking for finisher cards by the end of Act 2, or Act 3 will be difficult.

**Power cards** (by Act 3, you should be cycling your whole deck in ~4 turns):
- Devour Life, Haunt — power cards that defend you and deal damage just from playing
  Souls cards.
- Soul Storm — the ideal finisher once you've played tens of Souls in a fight.
- Death March, Spirit of Ash — useful when you have Souls and Energy to spare.

**Key relics:**
- Funerary Mask — adds 3 Souls to your draw pile at combat start; the single most
  important relic for this build.
- Anchor, Gorget, Horn Cleat — this build needs a few turns to get its Souls engine
  running, so early Block buys you the time to play your setup pieces safely.

**Playstyle notes:** Souls is less a standalone archetype than the engine that makes
Necrobinder's other two builds (Osty, Doom) function — most successful Necrobinder decks
run a Souls package underneath whichever payoff they've chosen.
