# Doom (Necrobinder)

**Goal:** Stack Doom on an enemy; once Doom reaches its HP, it dies at the end of its
turn. The archetype that needs the most support to feel good — unlike Poison, Doom only
kills at end of turn, so a "lethal" Doom stack still lets the enemy get one more attack
in. It's very workable even at high Ascension, but it takes real investment to click,
and is best approached once you have a wider unlock pool to draw from.

**Early priority cards:**
- Negative Pulse — a core Doom-application tool.
- Defy, Enfeebling Touch — don't apply Doom themselves, but weaken enemies during the
  turns you're trying to build the stack, buying survival time.
- Invoke, Borrowed Time, Delay — the better Doom cards cost real Energy, so pick up
  stalling tools that let you afford them.

**How Doom develops over a run:** Early on, you'll mostly apply Doom as a side effect of
picking up generically good cards — this is enough to pick off enemies once they're
under ~20 HP, and Act 1's low enemy HP pools make it a strong window for that. Resist the
temptation to build fully around Doom immediately; it needs structure first.

**Consistency cards** (Act 2 onward):
- Deathbringer, End of Days, Shroud, Reaper Form — expensive and hard to play. You'll
  need a strong Osty to tank damage, or reliable debuff-chaining to reduce incoming hits,
  before these become playable rather than risky.

**Power cards:** Once your Mana/Energy generation and card draw are solid heading into
Act 3, Doom becomes properly viable — landing Reaper Form safely usually means you have
what you need to close out most remaining fights.

**Key relics:**
- Book Repair Knife — heal 3 HP whenever you kill an enemy with Doom.
- Undying Sigil — halves damage from enemies whose Doom is at least equal to their HP;
  the build's two must-have relics.
- Lantern, Candelabra, Chandelier — Doom decks tend to run short on Energy, so
  Energy-generating relics are worth prioritizing whenever you find them.

**Playstyle notes:** Don't force a full Doom deck without the unlock pool to support it —
treat Doom as a bonus damage layer on top of a Souls/Osty foundation until the run
specifically hands you the pieces (debuffs, stalling tools, Energy) to make it a real
win condition on its own.
