# Necrobinder — Overview

**Core loop:** Osty (a companion), Doom, Souls, and corpse conversion.
**Starter relic:** Bound Phylactery or Phylactery Unbound — both Summon Osty at the
start of combat/turn in different amounts, depending on starting configuration.

## Playstyle summary

Necrobinder is the roster's highest-complexity, highest-ceiling class. It tracks more
simultaneous resources than any other character (Osty's HP, Doom stacks, Souls,
corpses, summon timing) and is unusually punishing when only part of the engine is
online — a half-built Necrobinder deck plays like an unsolved puzzle rather than a
functioning deck. When every piece lines up, it is also the most powerful class in the
roster.

## Early draft priorities

- Prioritize stable corpse and Soul generation before taking expensive payoff cards
  seriously — glue pieces matter more than headline cards here.
- Choose one lane (Doom execution vs. summon-heavy control) as soon as the run signals
  one; splitting the deck across half-built plans is worse on this class than on any
  other.
- If it's unclear whether "the engine is online yet," it almost certainly isn't — treat
  that uncertainty as a signal to keep drafting support pieces, not payoffs.

## Common mistakes

- Drafting flashy finishers before the deck can reliably fuel them.
- Assuming the engine is online because a few synergy pieces exist on paper, without a
  reliable generation loop backing them.
- Mixing too many packages (Doom + summons + sacrifice) together, producing turns full
  of ingredients but no actual payoff.

## Build paths

See `builds/` for the three main directions:
- `souls.md` — Necrobinder's card-draw engine; underlies most successful decks
  regardless of which payoff you choose.
- `osty.md` — the easiest build to pick up: grow your companion into a second health
  pool and damage source.
- `doom.md` — stack Doom for delayed kills; needs the most support before it clicks, best
  approached with a wider unlock pool.

Osty is the safest default build path — it covers Necrobinder's low-HP weakness
immediately. Doom is the highest-ceiling but slowest-to-stabilize line. Souls isn't
really a competing choice; run it underneath whichever of the other two you pick.
