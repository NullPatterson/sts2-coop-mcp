# Forge (Regent)

**Goal:** Play cards with the Forge effect to build up the Sovereign Blade, a 2-Energy
weapon with Retain that gets stronger with every Forge trigger and cycles back through
your deck after each use. The goal is a Sovereign Blade big enough to threaten multiple
enemies in a single swing.

**Early priority cards:**
- Wrought in War, Spoils of Battle, Furnace, Summon Forth, Seeking Edge, Bulwark — the
  core Forge toolkit; grab these as they appear.

**Playstyle caveat:** Forge has fewer dedicated support cards than the Stars line, and
it's genuinely difficult to build a deck around Forge alone — there just isn't the same
depth of support. The recommended approach is to add Forge cards to a deck that already
has a solid base, rather than committing to Forge from floor one. Some of the strongest
Forge payoffs also require Stars to be online, which is part of why this build works
best as a secondary line rather than a standalone plan.

**Key relics:**
- Fencing Manual — activates the Sovereign Blade at combat start with no card required;
  the single best relic for this line.
- Lunar Pastry — Forge decks still need some Star support to function, so this pulls
  double duty.
- Mini Regent — 1 Strength the first time you spend a Star each combat; pairs cleanly
  with this attack-heavy build.

**Playstyle notes:** Treat Forge as Regent's secondary weapon, not the main plan — layer
it onto a Stars-based foundation once that foundation is stable, rather than trying to
win purely off Sovereign Blade scaling.
