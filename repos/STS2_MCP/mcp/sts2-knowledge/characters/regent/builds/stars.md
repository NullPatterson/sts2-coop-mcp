# Stars (Regent)

**Goal:** Build Stars — a persistent resource that doesn't reset at end of turn and has
no upper cap — then spend them on powerful, low-Energy-cost cards. Regent starts every
fight with 3 Stars via the Divine Right/Divine Destiny starter relic, but you need to
generate more before the archetype's best cards pull their weight.

**Early priority cards** (build the resource before you build the payoffs):
- Gather Light, Glow, Hidden Cache, Solar Strike, Knockout Blow — most of these do double
  duty (generate Stars while also helping you survive), so even without an attack-based
  Stars card yet, they keep you alive to play Falling Star repeatedly.

**Consistency cards** (once Stars are flowing every turn):
- Astral Pulse, Comet, Genesis, Particle Wall, Crescent Spear, Reflect, Gamma Blast — the
  Stars line, unlike Forge, has real AoE options, which matters for the tougher elites
  and packs.

**Power cards** (finishers to chase by Act 3):
- Decisions, Decisions — the ideal Star sink: chains into itself to amplify high-cost
  Skills. Not required to make Stars work, but transformative if you get a copy or two.
- Royal Gamble, The Smith — combine to generate more Stars than you can spend, and to
  forge a dangerous Sovereign Blade on the side.
- Seven Stars, Stardust — Stardust is a strong finisher once Royal Gamble/Decisions,
  Decisions is generating tens of Stars per fight.

**Key relics:**
- Galactic Dust — 10 Block for every 10 Stars spent; a genuine game-changer once your
  turns start spending Stars in bulk (note: it triggers on *spending*, not banking).
- Lunar Pastry — 1 Star at end of turn; not deck-defining, but reliable filler value when
  your main Star payoffs aren't in hand.
- Oddly Smooth Stone — 1 Dexterity at combat start; this build is slow to come online
  through Act 2, so early survivability matters.
- Letter Opener — synergizes well since you'll be playing several Skills most turns.

**Playstyle notes:** It's normal for this build to feel weak until well into Act 2 — the
payoff is real, but it has to be earned by staying alive through the setup turns first.
