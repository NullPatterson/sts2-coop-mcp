# Regent — Overview

**Core loop:** Star, Sovereign Blade / Forge, and delayed burst.
**Starter relics:** Divine Destiny (gain 6 Star at combat start) or Divine Right
(gain 3 Star at combat start), depending on starting configuration.

## Playstyle summary

Regent is a delayed-payoff class: turns are allowed to look inefficient on purpose,
banking a resource (Star, or Forge) toward one large payoff turn rather than
trading constant small value. It rewards planning two turns ahead and punishes greedy
banking that leaves the fight already lost before the payoff lands.

## Early draft priorities

- Prioritize tools that build Star without falling behind on board state.
- Value cheap defense and consistency above flashy payoff early — bad setup turns are
  punished hard on this class.
- Upgrade the cards that make weak hands playable before chasing the dream version of
  the deck.
- The moment setup turns stop costing life/tempo is the signal the build is online —
  before that point, treat every banking turn as a risk, not a free action.

## Common mistakes

- Banking Star so greedily that the fight is already slipping away before the
  payoff arrives.
- Drafting spenders (payoff cards) without enough generation to actually support them.
- Treating a setup turn as a free action instead of something that has to be earned with
  real defense — most failed Regent runs come from getting too cute mid-fight, not from
  bad luck.

## Build paths

See `builds/` for the two main directions:
- `stars.md` — the default identity: bank Stars (a persistent, uncapped resource), cash
  them in on powerful low-cost cards.
- `forge.md` — build the Sovereign Blade via repeatable Forge triggers.

Stars is the primary archetype and has by far the deeper support pool, including
Regent's only real AoE options. Forge works best layered onto a Stars foundation rather
than as a standalone plan — there isn't enough dedicated Forge support to build a full
deck around it alone.
