# Orbs (Defect)

**Goal:** Channel enough Orbs that their passive effects alone carry your block and
damage each turn. Every Orb has a passive (triggers each turn) and an Evoke (a big
one-time effect that destroys the Orb): Lightning deals 3/evokes for 8 to a random
enemy; Frost gives 2 Block passively, 5 on Evoke; Dark adds 6 damage to its own Evoke
each turn it survives, then unloads on the lowest-HP enemy; Plasma gives 1/2 Energy;
Glass deals 4 AoE per turn (losing 1 value each time) or 8 AoE on Evoke. Cracked Core,
the starter relic, gives you a free Lightning Orb at combat start.

**Early priority cards:**
- Ball Lightning, Cold Snap — generate Lightning/Frost Orbs respectively at Strike-level damage.
- Lightning Rod — a strictly better Defend that also channels a Lightning Orb; an easy
  Act 1 include.
- Focused Strike — layer this in once you have several Orbs running to convert them into
  more damage.

**Consistency cards:**
- Glacier, Chill, Hailstorm — it's hard to build toward one specific Orb type, so aim
  for a balance of attack and block effects. A Frost-leaning deck is the safest overall
  choice, since you're rarely punished for having a bit less damage, especially at lower
  Ascension.
- Hotfix — temporarily increases Orb potency; note the effect only lasts one turn, so
  time it for a turn that matters.

**Power cards** (once you can freely build any deck you like):
- Capacitor — more Orb Slots to hold more simultaneous Orbs.
- Defragment — the best Focus card in the pool (Rare, so it won't show up often).
- Synchronize — a better, longer-lasting version of Hotfix.
- Voltaic — usually your finisher: channel tens (or hundreds) of Lightning Orbs across a
  fight, then unload with this card.

**Playstyle notes:** Focus scales every Orb's effect the way Strength/Dexterity scale
other classes — prioritize Focus sources once your Orb foundation is set. This is the
default, safest Defect line; only pivot to Claw if the run is clearly handing you that package instead.
