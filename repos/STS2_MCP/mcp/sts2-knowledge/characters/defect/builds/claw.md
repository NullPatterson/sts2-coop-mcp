# Claw (Defect)

**Goal:** Claw looks like a meme (a 0-cost Attack that deals only 3 damage), but built
correctly it becomes a legitimate primary damage plan: every time you play a Claw card,
all Claw cards in your deck deal 2 more damage this combat. It doesn't require dedicated
deck-building — it works fine even in a thin deck — but it's strongest when you can
consistently draw and replay it.

**Early priority cards:**
- Hologram — one of the best cards in the entire game and the central piece of this
  build: replay it whenever it lands in your discard pile.
- Scrape, Skim — synergize directly with Hologram and Claw, letting you play Claw
  multiple times in a single turn.

**Playstyle notes:** As long as you can generate enough Block through Frost Orbs to buy
time, you'll usually have room to stall and draw into your Claw pieces. This build pairs
naturally with a Frost-leaning Orbs foundation rather than replacing it — Frost buys the
time, Claw closes the fight.

**Where this build is weaker:** effectiveness drops noticeably at higher Ascension
levels compared to the core Orbs plan, so treat Claw as a strong Act 1–2 tool and a fun
alternate win condition rather than the default choice for a full climb.
