# Poison (Silent)

**Goal:** Apply stacking Poison and let it whittle the enemy down turn after turn while
you play defensively. A classic, reliable archetype — but a common mistake is overloading
your deck with Poison cards in Act 1, before you have the survivability to outlast a fight.
Build a defensive foundation first with cards like Dash, Backflip, and Footwork.

**Early priority cards:**
- Poisoned Stab, Bouncing Flask — strongest in Act 1; consider cutting them by late Act 2
  as their value drops off relative to better options.
- Noxious Fumes — worth using in nearly every fight, and even better once upgraded.

**Consistency cards** (once you can apply Poison reliably, pick a lane):
- Envenom — easy Poison application, pairs naturally with a Shiv sub-build.
- Corrosive Wave — pairs better with Sly, helping you reach cards like Bubble Bubble and Haze.

**Power cards:**
- Accelerant — the best Power card for this build, doubling Poison's trigger every time
  it fires.
- Mirage — an emergency valve, giving Block equal to total Poison applied across all
  enemies; note it carries Exhaust, so it's a one-shot tool per copy.

**Key relics:**
- Snecko Skull — +1 Poison every time you apply Poison; compounds fast, especially
  alongside Envenom and Shivs.
- Twisted Funnel — 4 Poison to ALL enemies at combat start, a strong floor against groups.

**Playstyle notes:** Poison wins by outlasting the fight, not by racing it — prioritize
your defensive foundation before leaning into the archetype, and expect this build to
shine most against single hard-hitting targets and long fights rather than fast group clears.
