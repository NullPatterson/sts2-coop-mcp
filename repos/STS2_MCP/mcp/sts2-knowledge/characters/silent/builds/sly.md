# Sly (Silent)

**Goal:** Sly cards play themselves for free when discarded, and Silent has more ways to
trigger discards than any other class. This is widely considered the strongest
individual archetype in STS2 — the other new mechanics don't come close — and it
naturally combines with both other Silent builds. The starting deck's only Sly activator
is Survivor, but that carries you through most of Act 1.

**Early priority cards:**
- Ricochet, Flick-Flack, Untouchable — all three Sly cards are worth their cost from Act
  1 onward and keep improving as you add more discard tools.

**Consistency cards:**
- Acrobatics — rifles through your deck and gives you an easy discard target for Sly.
- Dagger Throw — smooths awkward hands; underrated once Sly gives you a reason to run it.
- Prepared — ensures a playable hand while also feeding discard triggers.

**Power cards** (once loaded with discard tools, chase these uncommons/rares):
- Reflex, Tactician — high-cost cards that are obvious removal targets: Sly them and
  cycle your deck multiple times per fight.
- Hand Trick — opens a hybrid path into Poison or Shiv; Sly a card like Burst or
  Adrenaline and you're essentially free to play anything.
- Abrasive — a strong Sly enabler on its own.

**Key relics:**
- Bag of Preparation, Centennial Puzzle — card draw is essential to a Sly deck; these
  give you 2 extra cards at combat start and 3 on first HP loss, respectively.
- Letter Opener — 5 damage to ALL enemies for every 3 Skills played; comes up constantly.
- Tingsha — 3 damage to a random enemy per discard, which happens continuously in this deck.
- Tough Bandages — 3 Block per discard; stacks the same trigger toward survivability instead.

**Playstyle notes:** Sly plays well with both Poison and Shivs, so this is usually the
backbone you build everything else on top of rather than a standalone choice.
