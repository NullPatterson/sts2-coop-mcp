# Shivs (Silent)

**Goal:** Generate zero-cost Shiv attacks and chip enemies down with volume rather than
big single hits. Shiv decks are weaker than they used to be in the original game, but the
build is still viable, especially since STS2 doesn't throw the same density of
Shiv-punishing elites and bosses at you.

**Early priority cards:**
- Blade Dance — still worth taking: 12 damage for one Energy, and it exhausts, which can
  help in later acts.
- Cloak and Dagger — Silent struggles to build Block quickly on its own, so this is a
  reliable way to get Shivs and defense from the same card.
- Hidden Daggers — an excellent Sly activator that also hands you extra attacks; grab it
  early if you see it.
- Finisher — the combo piece that ends fights: strong even off a single Blade Dance, and
  absurd late-run once you can consistently cheat cards out.

**Power cards** (once you can reliably draw into your Shiv cards):
- Accuracy — turns every Shiv into a mini-missile once it's active; only include it once
  Shivs are showing up most turns.
- Fan of Knives — not essential, but helps in multi-enemy fights and works as a second
  Blade Dance at worst.
- Knife Trap — an alternate finisher, hitting an enemy once per Shiv in your Exhaust
  pile; effective even without tens of Shivs banked.

**Key relics:**
- Ninja Scroll — 3 Shivs into your hand at combat start; an easy, reliable pick for this deck.
- Helical Dart — 1 Dexterity every time you play a Shiv; can turn ordinary Defends into a
  serious source of Block once the deck is set up.

**Playstyle notes:** Shivs are a volume plan, not a burst plan — the build wants many
small, cheap actions per turn rather than a single explosive one. It pairs well with Sly
(Hidden Daggers, Blade Dance are both good discard targets) more than it pairs with Poison.
